Papers

Presented · proceedings forthcoming

Information Geometry of Prompt Quality: Representation Sensitivity as a Geometric Signal for LLM Behavior

Deepinder Singh, Abhinav Nara, Vishwanath Bijalwan*, Rajni Mohana

7th IEEE INDISCON 2026, MNIT Jaipur · Presented 12 Sept 2026

Asks whether the geometry of a language model's internal representations can signal prompt quality in a single forward pass, with no training. Tested on four open language models, eight prompt styles and 127 tasks.

Accepted

Categorical Verification Layer for Agentic AI: A Functional Calculus for Type-Safe Workflows

Gunakshh Sharma, Deepinder Singh, Vishwanath Bijalwan*

OCIT 2026 (24th OITS International Conference on Information Technology), NIT Kurukshetra · 17–19 Dec 2026

Proposes a verification layer that keeps security-critical decisions in typed code instead of trusting a language model's output, to protect AI agents from prompt-injection attacks.

* Corresponding author
